## Gates of Olympus Bonus Features



The gameplay in Gates of Olympus is fairly straightforward. There’s more than enough action here to keep things interesting though thanks to a Tumbling Reels game engine, random multipliers and free spins. Let’s run you through all of the bonus features, starting with the Tumbling Reels.



## Tumbling Reels



Land a winning combination and a Tumble follows. The successful symbols are blasted from the game grid and other symbols drop into the vacated positions and replace them. If this new combination creates a winner, you’ll get a payout and another Tumble follows. These continue until there’s a Tumble that doesn’t result in the creation of a new winning combination.



## Orb Multipliers



There are 4 special Orb symbols which Zeus can fling onto the reels at random. These come with multipliers attached. These payout boosts are worth 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 8x, 10x, 12x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x, 100x, 250x or 500x. When the Orbs are active, the multipliers are added together and applied to the total win from that series of tumbles. For example, say you landed a payout of £10 from a series of tumbles that includes 5x and 8x multiplier Orbs. The payout would be boosted by 13x, taking the award up to £130.



## Free Spins with Even More Multipliers



To trigger the bonus round, you need to land 4, 5 or 6 Zeus scatter symbols anywhere on the reels on the same spin. You’ll receive a payout equal to 3, 5 or 100 x bet before going on to play 15 free spins. These free spins begin with a standard 1x payout multiplier active.

Orb multipliers can appear during the free spins, just like in the base game. The big difference is they don’t reset following a winless tumble, they continue to rise throughout the round. You can keep track on the current value of the multiplier via the meter to the side of the reels.

Re-triggers are possible too. Another 5 free spins are added to your total if you land 3 scatters on the same spin. During testing, I only triggered this bonus feature once, which shows how hard it is to come back. Looking at the stats from the provider, the free spins feature will trigger once every 448 spins on average.
