## Reaction Wins



Central to much of the best gameplay on offer in Bonanza Megaways are the Reaction Wins. Land a winning combination and those successful symbols are blasted off the reels and new ones drop in and replace them. A new payout consideration is then made with the new symbols in place. If that results in another winner, the process is repeated. The Reactions keep on coming until there’s one where no new winning combination is created. The Reactions can really come into their own during the Free Spins feature which we’ll look at now.



## Free Spins with Expanding Multipliers



Spell out the word G.O.L.D with the scatter symbols during the base game to activate the game’s main bonus round. Your reward is 12 free spins. Any extra scatter that appears on the triggering spin add another 5 free spins to that total. The maximum available is 22.

The free spins action begins with payouts at the standard 1x multiplier rate. That figure can quickly rise though. Each time you land a Reaction win during the free spins, the value of that win multiplier increases by 1x. The multiplier doesn’t reset following a winless reaction either, it continues to rise throughout the round. There’s no cap on the multiplier either, it can keep on getting higher and higher.

Joining the action here is a new symbol. This trolley full of gold bars is a new scatter symbol which can land in the top position above the middle 4 reels. Land 3 or 4 of these on the same spin for a re-trigger. Another 5 or 10 free spins are then added to your total.
