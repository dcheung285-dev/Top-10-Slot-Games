## Razor Shark Bonus Features



The Mystery Stack feature is a stack of seaweed that can reveal matching symbols. The Mystery Stacks will nudge down one position after each spin until they are off the board.

When the Mystery Stack shows golden sharks, then the Razor Reveal feature activates. The position of the golden sharks will display multipliers or Scatter symbols.



## Razor Shark Free Spins



If you land three or more Scatter symbols you will get a Free Spins round. Once in the Free Spins round the second and fourth reels will have the Mystery Stacks which will keep moving down with each spin.

The Free Spins feature will continue as long as there is a Mystery Stack somewhere on the grid. There’s also a counter on the blue surfboard on the right that reflects the multipliers.
