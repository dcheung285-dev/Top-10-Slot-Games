## Free Spins



Land 3, 4 or 5 Fish Scatter symbols anywhere on the reels on the same base game spin and you’ll trigger the Free Spins feature. Respectively, your reward will be 10, 15 or 20 free spins.

As we mentioned earlier, the Fisherman enters the fray during the free spins. He’s wild and replaces all the standard symbols when he forms part of a winning line. He can also help you net some extra payouts. You’ll see that some of the fish symbols come with cash amounts attached. These are worth 2, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 50 or 2,000 x your total bet. Most of the time, these fish will harmlessly swim away without troubling the cashier.

That’s not the case when the Fisherman wild lands though. In that happy event, the Fisherman Wild gathers the amounts attached to all the fish on display on that free spin. These are then paid as a prize. Each Fisherman on display catches the fish prizes.

For example, land a free spin that contains fish with £5 and £10 attached but no wild and you’ll win nothing. Land those same fish symbol with 1 Fisherman Wild and you’ll win a £15 bonus. If there are 2 Fisherman Wilds on that free spin with the £5 and £10 fish, they each collect the prizes for a £30 payout.

Each time a Fisherman Wild lands, it is collected in a meter. Each time you collect 4 wilds, it delivers a retrigger and another round of 10 free spins is added to your total. Retriggers also bring multipliers into the mix. The first retrigger awards a 2x multiplier with 3x and 10x multipliers on offer if you manage to land 3 and 4 retriggers.
