## Sugar Rush Bonus Features



Sugar Rush has 4 bonus features. There are tumbles which lead to multiplier spots up to 128x. In the Free Spins feature, multiplier spots remain in position making it easier to achieve the bigger multipliers.



## Tumble



When you land 5 or more identical symbols on the grid, the Tumble feature comes into play. The winning symbols disappear from the grid with the remaining symbols falling to the bottom of the grid. Then the new empty positions are filled with new symbols falling from above. This allows for continuous wins. Tumbles continue until no more winning clusters are formed. By the way, this is a popular bonus feature in Megaways slots .



## Multiplier Spots



Moving on, winning symbols explode and mark a spot on the grid. After the second time a symbol explodes on the same spot, a multiplier is added. The multiplier begins at 2x and doubles every time 1 more symbol explodes in that position up to 128x. All marked spots with multipliers remain in play until the end of the tumbling sequence. They reset back to 1x when no more wins are formed.



## Free Spins



The Sugar Rush online slot comes with a Free Spins feature. It’s triggered by hitting 3 or more Rocket Gumball Machine Scatters anywhere on the grid. 3, 4, 5, 6 or 7 scatters will result in 10, 12, 15, 20 or 30 free spins respectively.

During the feature, and differently to the base game, marked spots and their multipliers remain in position until the end of the free spins. This means multipliers can double up to 128x with subsequent tumbles and free spins making it easier to benefit from multipliers.

Finally, landing 3, 4, 5, 6 or 7 scatters will reward you with 10, 12, 15, 20 or 30 extra free spins respectively. The number of free spins you can play with is unlimited.
