## Re-Spin Feature



This streak re-spin bonus can trigger at random following any base game spin. All examples of the symbol which has appeared the most often on that preceding spin are held. A re-spin is then made. If a new matching symbol lands, it too is held.

Also in play here are multiplier symbols which are also held and add another re-spin. The re-spins keep on coming until there’s one where no new matching symbol or multiplier hit. A final payout calculation is made, with the total amount boosted by the multiplier. If you’re lucky enough to land more than one multiplier during the re-spins, their values are combined to deliver an even bigger payout boost.



## Money Cart Bonus



To activate the game’s second feature, the Money Cart Bonus, you’ll need to land 3 or more Bonus symbols on the same. You’ll then head to a new screen made up of a new 6 reel, 4 row machine. The triggering Bonus symbols then reveal a value which will be a multiple of your bet size. You could also be awarded a Persistent Symbol which awards its modifier multiple times instead than just once.

An initial allocation of 3 spins is awarded. On every spin each symbol position can reveal a blank, a multiplier value or a character inspired modifier symbol. If you land only blanks, you lose a spin. Land a new symbol and the spins reset to three. The round ends when you run out of spins. If you fill a whole row with symbols, another reel is added, boosting your chances of landing a winner.

You can keep track of the current value of the payout in the box in the top right-hand corner of the screen. The prize is shown as a multiple of your bet size from the triggering spin.

The character symbols offer a wide range of modifiers. Here’s what the bandits can deliver:

- Collector – This symbol collects all the payout values on display on the other symbols on display and adds them to its own total.
- Persistent Collector – This symbol collects all the values on display elsewhere on the reels and adds them to its own total at the end of every spin. It doesn’t collect its own value though.
- Adjacent Collector – This collects the value on the 8 adjacent symbols and adds them to its own.
- Persistent Adjacent Collector – Land this to collect the values from the 8 adjacent symbols every spin.
- Payer – The Payer reveals a multiplier value which is then applied to the other symbols currently on the reels.
- Persistent Payer – This does the same as the Payer, adding multipliers, but it does so at the end of every spin that follows.
- Adjacent Payer – All adjacent symbols are boosted courtesy of a multiplier value.
- Persistent Adjacent Payer – A bet multiplier is applied to all symbols adjacent to this one at the end of the spin and on each subsequent spin.
- Sniper – The sniper takes shots at between 3 and 8 symbols and doubles their value. For example, a 2x symbol that was hit by a shot from the sniper would become 4x. The sniper can shoot the same symbol multiple times.
- Persistent Sniper – The sniper doubles the value of between 3 and 8 symbols at the end of every spin, not just the current spin. Collector Payer – In this combo modifier, 3, 4 or 5 symbols are targeted. The values are added to the Collector Payer’s own value. The updated totals are then awarded.
- Persistent Collector Payer – This does the same as the Collector Payer at the end of each remaining spin, not just the current spin.
- Arms Dealer – Between 1 and 4 Bonus symbols convert into feature symbols. Once converted the feature symbols award their modifier extra.
- Persistent Arms Dealer – One bonus symbol converts into a Feature symbol at the end of each spin.
- Necromancer – Between 2 and 7 single-use feature symbols that have already been used can be brought back to life courtesy of the Necromancer.
- Persistent Necromancer – Between 1 and 7 previously used Feature symbols are brought back to the life and awarded at the end of each remaining spin.
- Persistent Shapeshifter – This symbol turns into one of Collector, Payer, Sniper, Collector Payer, Arms Dealer or Unlocker on each remaining spin.
- Unlocker – A row that had lock becomes unlocked when this modifier is awarded.
- Reset Plus – This increases the number of spins.
- Upgrader – When this lands, between one and three feature symbols are upgraded to their Persistent version.
