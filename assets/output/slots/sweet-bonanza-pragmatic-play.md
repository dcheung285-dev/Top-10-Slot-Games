## Tumbles



Sweet Bonanza uses a Tumbling Reels game engine. When you land a winning combination, a tumble then follows. Those successful symbols are removed from the grid and new ones drop down to replace them. A new payout consideration then follows. If a new winner lands, another tumble follows. The tumbles keep on coming until there’s one where you don’t create a winner.



## Free Spins



To trigger the Free Spins feature, you’ll need to land 4, 5 or 6 Lollipop Scatters on a single spin. After pocketing an award equal to 3, 5 or 100 x your total bet, you’ll go on to play 10 free spins. The same Tumbling Reels mechanic is in play during the feature, just like the base game. There is one major difference in the free spins though.

Joining the action is a new symbol, the Rainbow Bomb Multiplier which comes with a multiplier attached. These multipliers can boost payouts between 2x and 100x. The multiplier is applied to all the tumbling wins that land on a single free spin.

For example, say you landed a winning combination that included a 10x Rainbow Bomb Multiplier, followed by 4 tumble wins. All the payouts from the original win plus the tumbles would be boosted by the 10x multiplier. Better still is if you land more than 1 Rainbow Bomb Multiplier during a single set of tumbles. The values of the multipliers are added together to give an even bigger payout boost.

Retriggers are possible too. Land 3 Lollipop Scatters on the same free spins and another 5 free spins are added to your total. This can happen various times for unlimited additional free spins.

To help trigger the Free Spins feature, there’s an Ante Bet feature which increases the bet size per spin by 25%. In return, extra Lollipop Scatters are added to the reels doubling the chances of triggering the free spins. Furthermore, and in some jurisdictions, players can use the Buy feature. It allows you to buy your way into the free spins for a fee of 100 x your total bet. However, this isn’t an option in the UK though.
