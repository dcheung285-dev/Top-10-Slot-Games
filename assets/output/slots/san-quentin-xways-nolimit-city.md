## Enhancer Cells



Above and below each reel are a couple of symbol positions that are locked behind bars. You can unlock these positions when you land 1 or 2 Bonus symbols. The Bonus symbols also become wild. A limited selection of symbols can land in Enhancer Cells. You could see any of the 5 higher-paying convicts, a wild, a Razor Split or an xWays symbol.

If you land a Razor Split symbol, all the symbols on that reel are sliced in two, doubling their number. A Razor Split in both the top and bottom Enhancer Cell quadruples the number of symbols. More symbols in turn means more ways to win. If every position on the reels is unlocked with Razor Splits, the number of ways to win reaches a mind-boggling 13,189,257,216 (that’s over 13 billion by the way).

If an xWays symbol lands in the Enhancer Cell, you’ll see stacks of 4 high symbols. These turn 8 symbols high if you land xWays in both Enhancer Cells.



## Split Wilds



Land a Split Wild and all the standard symbols on that reel turn into two. The only exception is the Split Wild itself which stays as a single symbol. The number of ways to win can top 46,000 with these in play.



## Lockdown Spins



Land 3, 4 or 5 Bonus symbols on the same spin to activate the Lockdown Spins feature. This will take approximately 230 spins to trigger on average. Before the feature begins, you’ll head to a new screen. This will determine how many free spins you’ll play, plus the multiplier. Both of these are chosen at random.

Regardless of how many free spins you receive, all the Enhancer Cells are active. You’ll also receive 1, 2 or 3 Jumping Wilds depending on how many scatters you landed on the triggering spin. Each Bonus symbol that lands awards more free spins and adds another Jumping Wild.

Jumping Wilds will jump to a random regular reel position for each free spin. Land a Jumping Wild on a reel with a Razor Split and the value of the multiplier is doubled. This multiplier will stay active for the rest of the round and can get as high as 512x.
