## Slot Features

## Free Spins



Catch 3 Paw Coin Bonus symbols and you will enter the Free Spins bonus round. Before the round starts you will get to pick one of two options: Sticky Wild Free Spins or Raining Wilds Free Spins.

If you choose Sticky option you can expect to get 7 spins and between 2 to 7 Sticky Wilds on each reel for 3 Bonus symbols, 12 spin and between 2 to 7 Sticky Wilds on each reel for 4 Bonus symbols, 15 spins and between 2 to 7 Sticky Wilds on each reel for 5 Bonus symbols or 20 spins and between 3 to 7 Sticky Wilds on each reel for 6 triggering Bonus symbols. Any time a Wild lands during the feature you can expect x1, x2 or x3 random multiplier to get applied.

If you choose Raining Wilds option you can expect to win 15, 18, 25 or 30 spins with up to 6 raining Wilds on each spin. The number of spins depend on the number of triggering Bonus symbols. During the round, each spin with up to 6 Wilds comes with a random x1, x2 or x3 multiplier.
