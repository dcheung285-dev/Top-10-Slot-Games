## Dead or Alive 2 Bonus Features



Dead or Alive 2 comes with a trio of bonus features. Triggered every 195 spins on average, all of them offer free spins complete with a special modifier extra or two. Each feature has a different level of volatility too.

Trigger the selector by landing 3, 4 or 5 Scatter symbols anywhere on the reels on the same spin. You’ll receive a payout equal to 4, 25 or 2,500 x your total bet before being offered the choice of the following 3 features.



## Train Heist Free Spins



Choose the Train Heist Free Spins feature and you’ll start with 12 free spins. Land a Wild symbol during and a multiplier increases in value by 1x as well as 1 more free spin being awarded. If you manage to get the multiplier up to a massive 16x, another 5 free spins are added to your total. This is the lowest variance option of the 3 features, offering regular but often small wins.



## Old Saloon Free Spins



Once again, 12 free spins are on offer in the Old Saloon Free Spins feature, this time with a 2x multiplier. Each time a Wild symbol appears, it turns sticky and remains in place for any free spins you have left. Land 5 sticky wilds and an extra 5 free spins are added to your total. This is the medium volatility choice, offering a balance between win size and frequency.



## High Noon Saloon Free Spins



Wilds are the key to success in the High Noon Saloon Free Spins feature. Like the other features, you’ll start the round with 12 free spins. As in the Old Saloon Free Spins, any wilds that land turn sticky. Land 2 or 3 Wilds on the same reel and they turn into 2x or 3x multiplier wilds.

Multiplier Wilds on different reels can combine to deliver some potentially enormous payout boosts. 5 extra free spins are added if you get Wilds on all 5 reels. It’s the High Noon Saloon Free Spins where 111,111 x bet max wins are up for grabs. Be warned though, this feature is the most volatile. They offer big potential wins but don’t expect to land a winning combination very often. This is the high-risk, high-reward option.
